## Rosters apply right away
- Picking a season, Today's rosters or My League from the in-game menu now reloads the game by itself. No more closing and reopening.
- After you install packs from the in-game Features screen, choose **Apply now** to load them.

## Player faces redone
- I checked every player on today's rosters against his official headshot and fixed skin tone, beards and hair for all 1,800+ of them. This covers a lot of players who had the wrong skin tone or looked nothing like themselves.
- All 1,509 custom faces were rebuilt, and 277 card portraits were re-rendered to match.
- A few players (mostly very tall ones) show the game's standard portrait for now.
- To get them: open the launcher, then **Disc & Features**, then **Update features**.

## Android (early build)
- Features download now works. The Features screen no longer says "No features available".
- Fixed the crash when picking a season in Leagues & Eras.
- The APK is under **Assets**. If something goes wrong, use **Send the last game log** on the setup screen, and let us know if it works for you.

## Updating
- **v1.4.2 or later:** choose **Install** in the launcher when v1.6.2 shows up.
- **Older versions:** download the package for your system under **Assets** and extract it.

Your disc, saves, settings and installed packs stay where they are.
